# Pilot localities

Newcastle and Lake Macquarie (NSW). A suburb is included when its centroid falls inside the City of Newcastle or
City of Lake Macquarie LGA polygon.

- Source: NSW Spatial Services, NSW_Administrative_Boundaries_Theme FeatureServer, layers 8 (LocalGovernmentArea)
  and 2 (Suburb), queried 2026-10-29.
- Count: 144 suburbs (51 Newcastle + 94 Lake Macquarie, less the Lake Macquarie water body which has no postcode).
- Match key against G-NAF is the locality name in NSW. Postcode is informational.
